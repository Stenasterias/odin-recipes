The dog is hot. It has been, always, hot? Or, was it, ever, not hot? I don't know. Hot dog, thusly.

Do you want to make the dog? Do you want the dog? Do it heat? Consume vigorously and perhaps often. Knowledge arises from the hotting dog: Hot, and always.

For now we make the dog, hotly. And so procure of thee:

  • 8 hot dogs
  • 1/2 cup garlic butter, divided
  • 8 hot dog buns
  • 2 cups shredded cheese

With aught in thy possession, pray, endeavour to diligently follow the steps hither:

  1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees F (200 degrees C). Score hot dogs, brush with garlic butter and place on a baking sheet
  2. Bake hotdogs in the preheated oven until nicely browned, 6 to 8 minutes.
  3. Meanwhile, brush insides of hot dog buns with garlic butter; toast buns in a large skillet over medium heat until golden brown. Set aside.
  4. In the same skillet, lay down 1/4 cup cheese and place a bun face down on top of the cheese. Cook undisturbed until cheese is melted. Remove from skillet and repeat with remaining buns.
  5. Place hot dogs in prepared buns and serve immediately.
